Controls
  • Tap the screen with the mouse or your finger, as with a stylus.
  • Type digits and names on the keyboard. On a phone, tap a field or abc/123.
  • Menu or tapping the title opens the menus. Ctrl+N/S/V are the command strokes.
  • Home quits to the launcher and saves the game, as on a real Palm.
  • Scroll buttons and the mouse wheel scroll Tips text.
